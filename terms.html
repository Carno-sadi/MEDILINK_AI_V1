4 Emergency SOS & Hardware Usage

The Medilink AI platform includes an Emergency SOS Button designed to assist users in rapidly initiating contact with emergency medical services during critical situations. Understanding the technical behavior and limitations of this feature is essential before reliance.

Technical Behavior of SOS Activation

Audio Alert

Triggers a high-decibel audio alarm through the device's speakers to alert nearby individuals.

Hardware Vibration

Activates sustained vibration patterns on supported devices for tactile emergency signaling.

SMS Routing

Redirects to the device's native SMS composer pre-filled with the Bangladesh 999 emergency number.

GPS Geolocation

Attempts to capture precise GPS coordinates to include in the emergency message body.

GPS Geolocation Consent: Upon activating the SOS Button, the platform will request access to your device's precise geolocation data. By triggering the SOS feature, you explicitly consent to the collection and transmission of your real-time GPS coordinates for the sole purpose of facilitating emergency response. This geolocation data is not stored on our servers and is only used within the emergency communication flow.

Limitations of the SOS Feature: The Emergency SOS Button relies on your device's hardware capabilities, operating system permissions, and network connectivity. Medilink AI does not guarantee that the SOS activation will successfully connect you to emergency services. Factors including but not limited to low battery, denied permissions, hardware malfunction, network unavailability, or operating system restrictions may prevent the feature from functioning as intended.

SMS Routing Clarification: The SOS feature redirects you to your device's native SMS application with the Bangladesh emergency number (999) pre-populated. You must manually press the send button within the SMS app to complete the emergency communication. Medilink AI does not directly send emergency messages on your behalf.

The SOS Button is provided as a supplementary convenience tool and does not replace the direct dialing of 999 or other locally available emergency numbers. We strongly recommend saving emergency contacts on your device independently of the Medilink AI platform.

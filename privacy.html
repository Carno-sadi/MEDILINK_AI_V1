Section 01

Information We Collect

At Medilink AI, we believe in radical transparency. We collect a focused set of user data — nothing more, nothing less — to ensure your healthcare journey on our platform feels intuitive, relevant, and deeply personal.

Every data point we gather serves a single purpose: to make your experience better. Below is a precise breakdown of what we collect and why.

  • Essential Profile Metrics Your name, age, location, and basic health profile parameters. These allow us to tailor the interface and surface age-appropriate, region-relevant health content from the moment you log in.
  • Preferred Medical Categories Specialties and health domains you interact with most frequently — such as cardiology, dermatology, or pediatrics — so we can prioritize relevant content, specialists, and resources on your dashboard.
  • Prescription Types & History An anonymized record of prescription categories you've explored or saved. This enables our AI to recommend complementary health services, refill reminders, and cost-effective pharmacy alternatives.
  • User Interactions & Behavior Patterns Click paths, session durations, and feature usage trends. These signals help us understand which parts of the platform serve you best — and which need refinement.
Section 02

How We Customize Your Experience

Everything we collect funnels into one mission: building a healthcare platform that feels like it was designed for you alone. Your data is never harvested for advertising or sold to brokers. It is analyzed — by our secure, on-platform AI — exclusively to improve your experience.

Why We Use Your Data

Your information is strictly analyzed to customize, personalize, and optimize every dimension of your Medilink AI experience. Here's what that looks like in practice:

  • Adaptive user interface that rearranges based on your most-used features
  • Doctor recommendations matched to your medical categories and history
  • Pharmacy suggestions surfaced by prescription type, location, and pricing
  • Optimized loading times and layout rendering based on past usage patterns
  • Proactive health content that aligns with your wellness interests

Our personalization engine runs entirely within Medilink AI's infrastructure. No third-party analytics platforms, no external ad networks, no cross-site tracking. Your behavioral data is processed in-house and used solely to make the platform smarter, faster, and more relevant for you.

When you open Medilink AI on a Monday morning, the dashboard you see is the product of your own preferences and interactions — not a generic template, and certainly not a profile built for advertisers.

Section 03

Data Security & Protection

Trust is the foundation of healthcare — and of our platform. We treat your data with the same rigor and duty of care that a medical professional applies to patient confidentiality.

End-to-End Encryption
All data in transit and at rest is encrypted using AES-256 and TLS 1.3 protocols — the same standards trusted by leading healthcare institutions worldwide.
Zero Third-Party Sharing
Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with unauthorized third parties. We do not participate in data brokerage of any kind — full stop.
SOC 2 & HIPAA Compliance
Our infrastructure meets SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA security requirements, ensuring continuous audit readiness and regulatory adherence.
Continuous Monitoring
Automated threat detection and real-time monitoring systems safeguard against unauthorized access 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

In the unlikely event of a data incident, we are committed to notifying affected users within 72 hours, in compliance with GDPR and applicable data protection regulations.

Section 04

Your Rights & Controls

Your data belongs to you. We've built straightforward controls into Medilink AI so you can manage your information with confidence and ease.

  • Access & Review — You can view all personal data Medilink AI holds about you at any time through your account settings.
  • Correction & Updates — Inaccurate or outdated information can be corrected instantly, ensuring your profile always reflects your current needs.
  • Deletion & Erasure — You have the right to request complete deletion of your account and associated data, processed within 30 days.
  • Data Portability — Export your data in a machine-readable format at any time — no questions asked, no delays.
  • Notification Preferences — Customize how and when Medilink AI contacts you. Opt out of non-essential communications without affecting core platform functionality.

We will never penalize you for exercising your rights. Limiting data collection may reduce personalization quality, but your autonomy always comes first.
